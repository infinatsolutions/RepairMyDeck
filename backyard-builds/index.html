FAQ

Do you build custom sizes?

Yes. Most backyard builds can be customized by size, material, finish, and layout.

What materials do you use?

We commonly use pressure-treated pine, cedar, exterior-grade screws, galvanized hardware, and outdoor-rated stains or sealers.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery may be available depending on your location and the size of the project.

Do you install the items?

Yes. Some items can be delivered and placed, while others can be installed or mounted depending on the project.

Are prices fixed?

Prices shown are starting prices. Final pricing depends on size, materials, finish, customization, delivery, and installation.

Can you match my deck?

Yes. Many items can be built to complement your existing deck, railing, stain color, or backyard style.

Can I send pictures of my space?

Yes. Sending photos helps us recommend the right size, layout, and design.

Can you add a bar to my existing deck?

Yes. We can quote simple railing bars, freestanding outdoor bars, or more custom deck-mounted and built-in bar options depending on the structure and available space.
